# INC0012861 — Legal Name and Email Update

**Environment:** Service Desk Simulator (training)  
**Status:** Account changes performed; independent final validation not documented.

## Request
An employee requested an update to their legal display name and primary email following a marriage, while retaining their previous email address as an alias.

## Actions performed
1. Verified the employee's identity under company security guidelines.
2. Consulted the internal Knowledge Base and followed documented procedures.
3. Opened Active Directory and updated the employee's name and email address.
4. Preserved the old email address as an alias.

## Outcome and verification
The account attributes and alias were updated according to the trainee's report. Separate end-to-end email delivery testing and recorded HR approval validation were not confirmed in the available notes.

## Skills
Identity verification; Active Directory user administration; email alias management; policy-based change handling; ticket documentation.

## Learning
A primary address change and alias retention are distinct requirements. Always confirm both, and record how they were tested.
