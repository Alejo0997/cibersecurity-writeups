# INC0012862 — Second Monitor Displays "No Signal"

**Environment:** Service Desk Simulator (training)  
**Priority:** Medium  
**Status:** Resolved (reported).

## Reported symptoms
The employee's secondary monitor powered on but displayed "No Signal", and the workstation did not detect it. The employee reported reduced productivity during month-end finance reporting.

## Checks already performed by the employee
- Confirmed cables were connected.
- Tried a different cable and another video port on the PC.
- Restarted the computer.
- Tested the monitor with another laptop; it worked.

These checks suggested the display itself was operational, but did not prove a specific software root cause.

## Actions performed
1. Verified the employee's identity under the company security process.
2. Consulted the internal Knowledge Base and followed its guidance.
3. Connected to the workstation via Remote Desktop.
4. Updated the video/display drivers.
5. Confirmed the second monitor worked again.
6. Recorded clear resolution notes in the ticket.

## Outcome
The monitor resumed working after the driver update. This supports a driver-related issue, though the exact underlying failure was not independently established.

## Skills
Remote desktop troubleshooting; graphics driver maintenance; peripheral diagnostics; evidence-based troubleshooting; incident documentation; user support.

## Learning
Use previous isolation tests before replacing hardware. Verify that the user-facing issue is resolved and document the result.
