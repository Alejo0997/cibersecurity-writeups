# Desktop Not Powering On — Hardware Troubleshooting

**Environment:** Service Desk Simulator (training)  
**Status:** In progress — not yet resolved.

## Reported symptoms
A customer support workstation had no lights, fan activity, or audible response when powered on. The user reported a working wall outlet and an inability to take queued calls.

## Actions performed so far

2. Consulted the company Knowledge Base and applicable procedures.
3. Asked the employee to confirm the power cable was securely connected.
4. The employee confirmed the cable was secure; the workstation remained unresponsive.
